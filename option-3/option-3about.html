Her story

Why KB & Associates exists.

Kerissa created KB & Associates Wealth Group to make important financial conversations feel more understandable, personal and responsible.

Her professional path has moved through military service, financial work and leadership. In each setting, preparation mattered. Details mattered. People depended on one another to communicate clearly and follow through.

Those principles now shape the client experience. Kerissa begins by understanding the individual, the family and the responsibilities behind the question. Only then can the right areas be identified for further review.

The objective is not to make financial planning sound impressive. It is to make the important issues easier to recognize, discuss and act on.
